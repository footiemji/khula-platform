Why we collect it

  • To assess affordability before offering credit, as required by the National Credit Act.
  • To verify your identity and reduce fraud — including confirming you control the phone number you're applying with, and that any payout account is genuinely yours.
  • To meet our obligations as a registered credit provider, including record-keeping and reporting requirements.
  • To communicate with you about your application and, if you take out a loan, payment reminders and confirmations.

We do not use your information for marketing without separate, explicit consent, and we do not sell your information to third parties.

How your information is protected

  • Identity, address, income, and bank account documents you upload are encrypted before they're stored (AES-256-GCM) and only accessible to authorised Khula staff reviewing your application.
  • Every time a staff member views one of your documents, or makes a decision on your application, it's logged against their name and the time — an audit trail, not just a policy statement.
  • Your ID number is never displayed back to you or anyone else in full through our systems once submitted; your account number is shown only in masked form (e.g. ****1234).

Your rights under POPIA

  • Access — you can ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction — you can ask us to correct information that's wrong or out of date.
  • Deletion — you can ask us to delete information we're not legally required to keep.
  • Objection — you can object to certain uses of your information.
  • Complaints — you can lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe we've mishandled your information.

WhatsApp specifically

When you message Khula on WhatsApp, that conversation is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy, since Meta processes the message on their platform to deliver it to us. We only use the WhatsApp Business Platform to run your loan conversation and send you updates about your own application — never for unsolicited marketing.
